Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Information we collect

When you shop with Wild Sage Apparel, information may be collected that is needed to provide the store and fulfill orders, such as your name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping details, order contents, and transaction-related information.

Payments

Payments are processed through Stripe. Wild Sage Apparel does not need to store your full payment-card number on the storefront server. Stripe may collect and process payment and fraud-prevention information under its own privacy practices.

Order fulfillment

Information needed to produce and ship an order may be shared with Printify, its print providers, carriers, and other service providers involved in fulfillment.

How information is used

We may use information to process and fulfill orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud or misuse, maintain the storefront, comply with legal obligations, and communicate with you about your purchase. Marketing messages are only sent when you have provided the information or consent required for that communication.

Cookies and technical data

The storefront and service providers may use cookies, local storage, logs, or similar technologies to keep shopping-bag information, operate checkout, maintain security, and understand site performance.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for business, support, accounting, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us with questions about personal information associated with your Wild Sage Apparel orders or communications. Certain records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Third-party services

The store currently relies on service providers including Render for hosting, Stripe for checkout and payment processing, and Printify and related providers for product fulfillment. Their own terms and privacy practices may also apply when they process information on our behalf.
