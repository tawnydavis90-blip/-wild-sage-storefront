THE WOMAN BEHIND WILD SAGE

Made for women who were never meant to fit in one box.

Wild Sage Apparel was built around one simple idea: you can be soft and strong, grounded and wild, lighthearted and dark around the edges—all at the same time.

Founder noteWear your contradiction.
Own every part of it.

I created Wild Sage because I wanted clothing that felt more like a person than a trend. A little boho, a little dark, a little rebellious, and still feminine. Pieces for the woman who can love wildflowers, loud music, tattoos, road trips, quiet mornings, and a little chaos without needing any of it to make sense to anyone else.

This brand is personal. It comes from learning to choose yourself, protect your peace, hold higher standards, and still stay open to joy. Wild Sage is for women who have lived enough life to know that strength does not always look hard, and softness does not mean weakness.

“You do not have to water yourself down to be easier to understand.”

I wanted the store to feel like the same contradiction the clothes represent: earthy but bold, laid-back but intentional, a little witchy, a little hippie, and unapologetically individual. Some designs are playful. Some are darker. Some say exactly what you are thinking. They all belong here.

Wild Sage is not about becoming somebody different. It is about wearing what already feels like you.

Wild at heart

Made for independent souls, spontaneous plans, and women who refuse to live small.

Higher standards

Choosing quality, intention, boundaries, and the things that genuinely feel right.

Beautifully real

No perfect boxes. No watered-down personalities. Just the whole complicated person.
