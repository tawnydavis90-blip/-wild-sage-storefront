Returns & Replacements

Last updated September 2026

Damaged, misprinted, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, defective, or you receive the wrong item, contact us promptly with your order information and clear photos of the issue. We will review the problem and, when appropriate, arrange a replacement or other resolution.

Made-to-order products

Wild Sage Apparel products are generally made specifically after you order them. Because of that, we do not automatically accept returns or exchanges for buyer’s remorse, incorrect size selection, or a change of mind.

Size and color

Please review the product details and available options before checkout. Screen settings and manufacturing variations can cause slight differences in color. If an item is materially different from what was ordered, contact us and we will review it.

Do not send items back without approval

Please contact us before mailing anything. Returning an item to a fulfillment facility without instructions may prevent us from locating or processing it.

Refund timing

If a refund is approved, it will be issued to the original payment method. Banks and card issuers may take additional time to post the credit after it has been processed.
