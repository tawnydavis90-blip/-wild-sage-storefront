How do payment and deposits work?

Submit your request first. Our team checks stock and confirms your final price and deposit terms before providing a secure Stripe payment link. Any security deposit is separate from rental fees and refunded after return and inspection, subject to your agreed terms. Card holds and automatic damage charges are not enabled.

What does a week cost?

Totes are $3.75 each per week, with a 10-tote minimum. Twenty totes cost $75. Delivery and pickup together add $50 once per booking. Each dolly adds $10 per rental week. Twenty totes, one dolly, and delivery for one week total $135 before any applicable taxes.

Where do you deliver?

Contact us to confirm service for your delivery and return addresses.

Can pickup be at my new address?

Yes. Enter your starting address for delivery and your destination for pickup. Both addresses must be within our confirmed service area. Delivery and pickup cost $50 combined per booking.

What can I use the totes for?

Moving apartments or homes, college move-ins, office moves, temporary storage, and organizing. Choose the tote count that fits your belongings; we do not assign a guaranteed bedroom capacity to each package.

Can I keep the totes longer?

Contact us before your scheduled return. Extensions require availability and confirmation from our team.
